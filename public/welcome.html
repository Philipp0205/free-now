How it works

Three steps.

  1. 1

    Start a circle

    Pick a name for yourself and for your circle.

  2. 2

    Invite your friends

    Share the invite link or code. Only people who have it can join.

  3. 3

    Say you're free

    Tap when you're free now, or make a plan for later. Friends tap to join.

What you get

Small, private, quick.

  • Free now

    One tap tells your circle you're around. Friends answer Join, On my way, or Can't make it.

  • Plans for any day

    Today, tomorrow, or a date up to a year ahead. Everyone sees who's in and who's a maybe.

  • A chat for each plan

    Sort out the details next to the plan, not in a group chat that scrolls away.

  • Places, if you want

    Add a meetup spot and see it on the map. Hangry does not track your location in the background.

  • Notifications

    On a phone, add Hangry to your home screen and turn on notifications to hear when friends are free.

  • Your calendar

    Save a plan to your calendar, or subscribe to a private feed of your plans.
