Where to meet

Pins are meetup spots, not people.

Add a place when you say you're free, or leave it out. Friends see the spot on the map and can open it in their maps app.

  • A pin is a place someone chose, or one reading of their phone's location rounded to about 100 metres, only if they tap for it.
  • Hangry does not follow anyone's location in the background.
  • A free-now pin is cleared when it ends. A plan's pin is cleared after its day.

How it works

Three steps.

  1. 1

    Start a circle

    Pick a name for yourself and for your circle.

  2. 2

    Invite your friends

    Share the invite link or code. Only people who have it can join.

  3. 3

    Tap the buzzer

    Tap the glowing buzzer, add a note or a place if you like, and press I'm free. Friends answer Join, On my way, or Can't make it.

Friends who added Hangry to their home screen and turned on notifications get a push. Everyone else sees it under Alerts the next time they open the app.
