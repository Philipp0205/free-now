Privacy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Hangry is an invite-only app for a private circle of friends. This page says what we store and why. We do not show ads, and we do not sell your data.

Your account

Hangry is invite-only. You join with an invite code, or you start a circle and invite others. There is no public signup.

We store the name you choose and, if you add one, your photo. We store a hashed session token for the device you sign in on, and a hashed recovery code so you can sign back in if you clear the site. We also store your status, such as free, busy, or away, plus any note, place, and end time you set, your timezone, and weekly busy hours if you set them.

Every account has an email address. We store it, and the time you confirmed it, so you can get a sign-in link if you lose your recovery code. We email you only for that: a link to confirm the address, and a link to sign back in when you ask for one. Those links work once and expire. We do not send marketing mail and we do not share the address. Emails go out through our mail provider, Resend.

Circles, plans, and chat

We store the circles you belong to, their names and invite codes, and who is in them.

If you start or join a social or a plan, we store the note, description, date or time, who is in, and any place you attach. Chat messages, replies, and reactions in your circles are stored too.

Location

We do not track your location in the background. A place is stored only if you add one to a social or a plan, including a one-time reading from your phone if you choose that, rounded to about 100 metres. Friends see the place name. Notifications do not include coordinates. When a social expires, its place and coordinates are deleted. For a plan, the pin is cleared after the day, and the typed place name stays.

Notifications

If you turn on push notifications, we store a subscription for your device so we can send alerts. We also store the alerts themselves so you can read them in the app.

Calendar

If you paste a private calendar link, we keep that link encrypted on the server. We store busy times, and we store your event titles encrypted for you alone, so you can see them when you tap I'm in. Friends only see that you are busy or free. Places, notes, and people from that file are discarded.

If you connect Google Calendar, Hangry asks Google only for the calendar.freebusy permission, which is busy and free times. Hangry never receives event titles or details, and never stores them. We store only your busy or free blocks for the coming days, and an encrypted token on the server so we can check again. If you disconnect, we delete the token and the busy times, and we tell Google to revoke access.

You choose which circles can see your busy times.

Assistant tokens

If you create a token for an assistant, we store only a hash of it, plus the name you gave it. The full token is shown once. You can revoke it at any time, and it stops working at once.

Feedback

If you send feedback in the app, we store the text, your account, the circle you had open, and the app version.

Where it is hosted

Hangry runs on Railway in the EU, in the europe-west4 region.

Deleting your data

There is no self-serve delete button yet. To delete your account and the data we hold for you, ask in the app with Send feedback, or contact the admin. We will delete it.

Contact

Questions about this policy: ask in the app, or contact the admin.
